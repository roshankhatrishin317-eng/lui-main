# About Page Blueprint — The Lums Charms

## Purpose
Introduce The Lums Charms brand story, highlight the salon environment, and showcase the talented team behind the artistry, building trust and emotional connection with visitors.

## Audience Objectives
- Understand the salon’s origin, mission, and unique approach.
- Meet the technicians and learn about their specialties.
- Gain confidence in salon hygiene, certifications, and hospitality.
- Discover press features and community involvement.

## Page Structure
1. **Brand Narrative Hero** — Story-focused headline, supporting paragraph, CTA to booking.
2. **Origin Story** — Timeline or narrative blocks describing founding inspiration and values.
3. **Team Gallery** — Grid of technician cards with bios, specialties, certifications, social links.
4. **Salon Experience** — Image carousel or video tour showcasing ambience.
5. **Values & Practices** — Icons with short descriptions (e.g., Hygiene, Inclusivity, Artistry, Wellness).
6. **Press & Recognitions** — Logo strip, testimonials from media or influencers.
7. **Community & Events** — Stories of collaborations, workshops, or charity events.
8. **CTA Section** — Invitation to book or contact for partnerships.

## Data Dependencies
- Team member bios from CMS (`technician` documents).
- Press mentions, awards, community highlights.
- Salon imagery and hero assets from photography shoot.

## Components Used
- `SectionHeading`
- `GlassCard`
- `TeamCard`
- `ImageCarousel`
- `ValueIconList`
- `GlowButton`

## Interactions
- Hover states on team cards to reveal fun facts.
- Scroll-triggered timeline animations for origin story.
- Carousel with autoplay (pause on hover) for salon experience.

## SEO & Metadata
- Title: “About Us | The Lums Charms Nail Salon Team & Story”
- Meta description: Emphasize artistry, certifications, salon atmosphere.
- Structured data: `Organization` with `member` references for technicians.

## Analytics Events
- `team_card_open`
- `about_press_link_click`
