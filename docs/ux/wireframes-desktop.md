# Desktop Wireframes — The Lums Charms

## Home (Desktop)
- **Hero Section**
  - Full-width background video showcasing nail artistry.
  - Left-aligned heading and subheading with `Book Now` and `View Portfolio` buttons.
  - Right column emphasizes featured look with rotating spotlight.
  - Navigation: sticky glassmorphic bar with menu items (`Portfolio`, `Services`, `About`, `Blog`, `Book Now`).
- **Signature Services**
  - Three-column cards with icon overlays, short descriptions, price, and “See Details” link.
- **Portfolio Showcase**
  - Interactive gallery using masonry layout with hover states revealing technician and tags.
  - Secondary CTA: “Filter by Style”.
- **Testimonials & Reviews**
  - Carousel with simultaneous display of two testimonials, star ratings, and client photo.
- **Salon Experience**
  - Split section: copy on left, imagery stack on right with subtle parallax.
- **Newsletter & Social Proof**
  - Sign-up form over soft gradient, social icons, user-generated content strip.
- **Footer**
  - Four-column layout: Quick Links, Contact, Hours, Newsletter.

## Portfolio (Desktop)
- **Filter Panel**
  - Left sidebar with filter groups (Style, Occasion, Finish) and search.
- **Gallery**
  - Three-column grid with hover zoom and quick-view button.
  - Lightbox displays large image, description, service bundle CTA.
- **Highlights Banner**
  - Horizontal strip featuring seasonal collection with link to blog post.

## Services (Desktop)
- **Intro Grid**
  - Two-column layout introducing signature bundles and add-ons.
- **Detailed Cards**
  - Accordion cards spanning full width with imagery on left, details on right.
- **Pricing Table**
  - Comparison of Classic, Premium, and Charm packages.
- **FAQs**
  - Two-column accordion with icons.
- **Booking CTA**
  - Full-width bar with button and secondary link to policies.

## About (Desktop)
- **Brand Story**
  - Hero narrative with supporting video and pull-quote.
- **Team Gallery**
  - Grid of technician cards with modal details (specialties, certifications, socials).
- **Salon Tour**
  - Scroll-triggered image gallery with captions.
- **Press & Awards**
  - Logo strip of media features.

## Booking (Desktop)
- **Scheduler Embed**
  - Central column with responsive calendar and service list.
- **Guidance Sidebar**
  - Right column containing preparation tips, policies, contact link.
- **FAQ Drawer**
  - Collapsible panel underneath embed.

## Blog (Desktop)
- **Hero**
  - Featured article with large image and “Read Now”.
- **Grid**
  - Three-column card layout with category labels and read time.
- **Category Filters**
  - Sticky top bar when scrolling.
- **Newsletter**
  - Inline subscription block between article rows.

## Contact (Desktop)
- **Two-Column Layout**
  - Left: contact form (name, email, phone, message).
  - Right: map embed, hours, quick action buttons.
- **FAQs & Policies**
  - Accordion referencing key policies below form.

## Global Design Patterns
- Generous whitespace, soft drop shadows, and layered gradients.
- Hover states include glow-border highlights and subtle scale-up.
- Animations triggered on scroll (fade-up, slide-in) with 250ms ease-in-out.
- Sticky `Book Now` button persists at top-right.
