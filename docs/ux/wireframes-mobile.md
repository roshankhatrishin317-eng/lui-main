# Mobile Wireframes — The Lums Charms

## Home (Mobile)
- **Hero Section**
  - Background: soft gradient `#BFA2FF → #FFC7D3` with floating sparkles.
  - Elements: logo top-left, hamburger menu top-right, headline, subheading, `Book Now` pill button.
  - CTA bar anchored at bottom for quick booking.
- **Signature Services Carousel**
  - Horizontal scroll cards with icon, title, duration, price.
  - “View All Services” button at end of carousel.
- **Portfolio Preview**
  - Two-column masonry thumbnails with tap-to-expand lightbox.
  - Overlay tag (e.g., “Bridal”, “Chrome”).
- **Testimonials**
  - Single card slider with avatar, rating, quote snippet.
- **Salon Story**
  - Split stacked blocks: image followed by copy and `Meet the Team` link.
- **Booking CTA Strip**
  - Sticky gradient bar with `Reserve Your Spot` button.
- **Footer**
  - Quick links, social icons, contact info, newsletter form.

## Portfolio (Mobile)
- **Filter Drawer**
  - Chip list (All, Bridal, Seasonal, Statement, Minimalist, Custom Charms).
  - Sort dropdown (Newest, Most Loved).
- **Grid**
  - Two-column tile layout with image ratio 4:5.
  - Tap reveals lightbox with description, technician, service tags.
- **Callout**
  - Inline CTA: “Want this look? Book with [Technician]”.

## Services (Mobile)
- **Intro Block**
  - Highlight top packages with icons.
- **Accordion List**
  - Each service card: name, duration, price, description.
  - Sub-accordion for add-ons (e.g., nail art, gel removal).
- **Policies Snapshot**
  - Three icon bullets for cancellation, deposits, hygiene.
- **CTA**
  - Primary button to `Booking`, secondary to `FAQs`.

## About (Mobile)
- **Brand Story**
  - Video thumbnail leading to modal.
- **Team Profiles**
  - Card stack: photo, name, specialty, certifications.
  - “See Portfolio” link per technician.
- **Salon Environment**
  - Image carousel with captions (e.g., “Tea Lounge”, “Charm Wall”).

## Booking (Mobile)
- **Embedded Scheduler**
  - GlossGenius iframe responsive within card.
  - Pre-populated instructions and deposit note.
- **FAQs**
  - Expandable list covering preparation, arrival, reschedule.
- **Support CTA**
  - Secondary button to `Contact` page or WhatsApp link.

## Blog (Mobile)
- **Category Pills** sticky on scroll.
- **Article Cards** featuring cover image, tag, reading time, excerpt.
- **Newsletter Module** mid-page encouraging tips subscription.

## Contact (Mobile)
- **Quick Action Buttons**: Call, WhatsApp, Email.
- **Map Embed** with directions button.
- **Hours & Location** stacked cards with iconography.
- **Contact Form** multi-field with progress indicator.

## Patterns
- Sticky bottom CTA for booking on key pages.
- Scroll-to-top button appears after 50% scroll depth.
- Micro-interactions: button press depth, card shimmer on appear.
