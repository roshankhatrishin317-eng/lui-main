# Copywriting Brief — The Lums Charms

## Tone & Voice
- **Tone**: Friendly, enchanting, confidence-inspiring.
- **Voice**: Speak directly to the reader (“you”), emphasize pampering, glamour, and meticulous care.
- **Personality Traits**: Warm, whimsical, attentive, professional.
- **Vocabulary**: glow, charm, shimmer, bespoke, pamper, luxe, artistry.
- **Avoid**: Overly formal language, technical jargon, clichés.

## Key Messages
- Every set is a custom piece of art tailored to your style.
- The salon prioritizes hygiene, comfort, and premium products.
- Booking is effortless with flexible scheduling options.
- The team is experienced, certified, and trend-aware.
- The salon offers exclusive collections, seasonal designs, and loyalty perks.

## Page-by-Page Guidance
- **Home**
  - Headline capturing glamour + confidence.
  - Subheading delivering value proposition and emotional hook.
  - Service snippets focusing on benefits (e.g., “Enchanting details in every stroke”).
  - Testimonials: highlight transformation stories and trust.
  - Calls to action: “Book Your Glow”, “Explore Signature Looks”.

- **Portfolio**
  - Intro copy describing customization and versatility.
  - Style filters with enticing descriptions (“Bridal Romance”, “Bold Statement Charms”).
  - Lightbox captions: include inspiration, materials, care tips.

- **Services & Pricing**
  - Clear explanations, highlight outcomes and experience.
  - Transparent pricing and add-on descriptions.
  - Policy snapshots using reassuring language.

- **About**
  - Brand story: origins, values, community involvement.
  - Team bios: highlight specializations, certifications, personal touch.

- **Booking**
  - Clear guidance on scheduling process (steps, deposit info, prep guidelines).
  - Friendly reminders about arrival times and policies.

- **Blog**
  - Educational tone for care tips, upbeat energy for trend features.
  - CTAs at end of posts: “Book the look” or “Subscribe for more sparkle.”

- **Contact**
  - Provide reassurance for quick responses, multiple contact options.
  - Encouraging message for special requests or accessibility needs.

- **Policies & FAQs**
  - Use positive, respectful language even for strict policies.
  - Reiterate hygiene and client well-being focus.

- **Promotions & Gift Cards**
  - Emphasize exclusivity and urgency (“Limited-time charm collection”).

## Content Assets Needed
- Testimonials (names, quotes, services received).
- Technician bios (experience years, specialties, fun fact, certifications).
- Service descriptions (what’s included, duration, aftercare).
- Brand story key points (founding date, inspiration, community focus).
- Policies (cancellation, deposits, hygiene practices, accessibility).

## Style Tips
- Use short paragraphs and punchy lead-ins.
- Incorporate playful microcopy (e.g., button tooltips, form placeholders).
- Highlight benefits before features.
- Use inclusive language; avoid assumptions about gender or background.

## Approval Workflow
1. Copywriter drafts content following brief.
2. Creative director reviews for brand alignment.
3. Operations verifies accuracy of services, pricing, policies.
4. Final approval by executive team before publish.
