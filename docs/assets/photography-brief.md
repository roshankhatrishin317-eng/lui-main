# Photography Brief — The Lums Charms

## Objectives
- Capture the luminous, playful essence of The Lums Charms salon.
- Provide versatile imagery for hero sections, portfolio showcases, team bios, and social channels.
- Ensure consistent lighting, color temperature, and composition styles.

## Deliverables
- **Hero Video Loop** (10–15s) of nail artist applying charms with soft focus bokeh.
- **Salon Ambience Stills** (10 shots): reception, lounge, charm wall, sterilization area, lighting fixtures.
- **Technique Macro Shots** (15 shots): close-ups of nail art, brushes, charms, polish pours.
- **Before & After Sets** (5 sequences): bare nails to finished designs with consistent framing.
- **Team Portraits** (1 per technician): waist-up, natural smiles, optional tool props.
- **Lifestyle Imagery**: clients sipping tea, hand accessories, detail shots with jewelry.

## Style Guidelines
- **Lighting**: Soft, diffused light; avoid harsh shadows; color temperature ~5000K.
- **Color Palette Alignment**: Use backgrounds/props in pastel tones (`#BFA2FF`, `#FFC7D3`, `#A8F0D1`).
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, negative space for text overlays, mix of portrait and landscape orientation.
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow focus on nail details, ensure secondary elements are tastefully blurred.
- **Consistency**: Maintain similar exposure and editing presets across sets.

## Shot List
1. Hero close-up of nail charm placement with shimmer effect.
2. Nail artist applying top coat with precise brushwork.
3. Charm wall detail featuring curated accessories.
4. Cozy lounge seating with branded pillows.
5. Manicure station ready for service (sterile tools, towels).
6. Team group shot in front of salon feature wall.
7. Technician portrait with naming board or charm tray.
8. Client hand resting on pastel surface showcasing rings and charms.
9. Polaroid-style display of past designs.
10. Product shot of charm collection in branded dish.

## Equipment & Props
- DSLR or mirrorless camera with macro lens (e.g., 100mm).
- Reflectors, softbox lighting, diffusion panels.
- Props: curated jewelry, pastel trays, branding elements, fresh flowers.

## Post-Production
- Retouch for skin tone consistency and nail detail clarity.
- Export formats: RAW, high-res JPG, WebP for web optimization.
- Provide color correction preset for ongoing content creation.

## Timeline
- **Pre-Production**: 1 week to finalize shot list, schedule models, prep salon.
- **Shoot Day(s)**: 1–2 days depending on availability.
- **Editing Delivery**: 7–10 days post-shoot with review round.

## Approvals
- Creative director to review moodboard and shot list before shoot.
- Final assets approved by brand manager and web design lead.
