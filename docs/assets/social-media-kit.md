# Social Media Launch Kit — The Lums Charms

## Objectives
- Generate buzz for the new website launch and salon services.
- Drive traffic to the booking page and grow social community.
- Encourage user-generated content through hashtags and incentives.

## Key Campaign Dates
- **Teaser Phase**: T-7 to T-4 days
- **Launch Phase**: T-3 to Launch Day
- **Sustain Phase**: Launch Day to +14 days

## Brand Hashtags
- Primary: `#TheLumsCharms`
- Secondary: `#CharmYourNails`, `#LuminousMani`, `#NailArtMagic`

## Content Pillars
- **Behind the Scenes**: Sneak peeks of salon spaces and styling prep.
- **Signature Looks**: Highlight flagship nail designs and seasonal collections.
- **Team Moments**: Introduce technicians, share fun facts, celebrate expertise.
- **Client Glow-Ups**: Before/after transformations, testimonials, UGC reposts.
- **Tips & Education**: Nail care routines, charm maintenance, product spotlights.

## Platform Strategy
- **Instagram**
  - Reels showcasing hero video footage, charm placement, salon ambience.
  - Carousel posts for portfolio highlights and service explanations.
  - Stories with countdown sticker for launch, polls (e.g., “Which charm is your vibe?”).
  - Link in bio updated to new booking page; use link stickers.
- **TikTok**
  - Fast-cut videos of charm application, trend remixes, technician spotlights.
  - Voiceovers explaining service benefits, trending audio for reveals.
- **Pinterest**
  - Boards organized by style (Bridal, Statement, Minimalist, Seasonal).
  - Rich pins linking to blog articles and portfolio categories.
- **Facebook**
  - Event for launch celebration with RSVP link.
  - Long-form post summarizing services, testimonials, and booking CTA.
- **Email Newsletter**
  - Countdown teaser, launch announcement, and follow-up exclusives.

## Launch Day Toolkit
- **Hero Copy**: “Our new digital home is live—book your glow and browse our luminous nail art portfolio.”
- **CTA**: “Tap to explore & reserve your charm session.”
- **Story Sequence**: 1) Hero video teaser, 2) Swipe through service highlights, 3) Booking CTA with link sticker.
- **Influencer Kit**: Personalized DM template, curated photos/videos, discount code for followers.

## Visual Guidelines
- Use gradient overlays (`#BFA2FF → #FFC7D3`) and charm motifs to frame imagery.
- Apply consistent typography (`Playfair Display` headlines, `Inter` body) for reels/text overlays.
- Add subtle motion graphics (sparkle animation, shimmer transitions).

## Engagement Plan
- Run giveaway: “Win a Charm Session” (tag friend + share story).
- Encourage clients to share nails with hashtag; repost to stories.
- Host live Q&A featuring lead technician discussing trends.

## Measurement & KPIs
- Instagram reach & engagement rate.
- Click-through rate to booking page from link in bio.
- Newsletter sign-ups from social promo.
- Number of UGC posts with branded hashtag.

## Responsibilities
- **Content Creation**: Social media manager + photographer.
- **Scheduling**: Use Later or Buffer to queue posts.
- **Community Management**: Reply to comments/DMs within 12 hours.
- **Reporting**: Weekly dashboard summarizing metrics, insights, and next steps.
