| Salon | Strengths | Weaknesses | Opportunities for The Lums Charms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GlamTips Studio** | Strong social presence, premium imagery, integrated booking | Overloaded UI on mobile, slow gallery load times, limited testimonials | Deliver faster, more focused mobile experience with curated highlights |
| **Polish & Poise** | Transparent pricing, detailed hygiene section, blog insights | Visual brand feels dated, minimal video content, no influencer partnerships | Lean into modern visuals, hero video loops, and co-marketing with creators |

## Industry Insights
- Mobile bookings now compose over 60% of salon reservations; responsive performance is critical.
- Unique nail art and specialty finishes (chrome, encapsulated charms) drive social engagement.
- Clients expect transparent sanitation protocols post-pandemic; highlight processes prominently.
- Influencer collaborations and user-generated content significantly expand reach.

## Positioning Opportunities
- Spotlight signature “charm” services with storytelling and before/after comparisons.
- Leverage ambient video to convey the salon’s playful-luxe vibe.
- Provide guided collections (e.g., bridal, seasonal, statement looks) for faster decision-making.
- Implement a cohesive loyalty narrative (newsletter tips, exclusive offers) within the experience.
