# Audience Personas — The Lums Charms

## Persona 1: The Trendsetting Professional
- **Demographics** Female, 26–34, urban professional, mid-to-high income.
- **Motivations** Wants polished, photo-ready nails that align with fashion trends; seeks a relaxing self-care routine after work.
- **Goals** Quick booking, luxe experience, trustworthy hygiene, shareable results for Instagram.
- **Pain Points** Long waitlists, unclear pricing, inconsistent quality, salons that don’t respect time.
- **Key Messaging** “Express your style with dazzling nails crafted around your schedule.”
- **Preferred Channels** Instagram, TikTok, email newsletters, Google search.

## Persona 2: The Celebration Planner
- **Demographics** Female, 22–40, engaged/newlywed or planning special events (baby showers, parties).
- **Motivations** Coordinated looks for bridal parties or themed events, wants packages for groups.
- **Goals** Reliable scheduling for multiple people, cohesive aesthetic, memorable treat for friends.
- **Pain Points** Unclear group pricing, lack of availability, salons with limited creative range.
- **Key Messaging** “Turn your milestone into a sparkling memory with custom nail art for every VIP.”
- **Preferred Channels** Pinterest, wedding blogs, Facebook groups, referrals.

## Persona 3: The Self-Care Enthusiast
- **Demographics** Female-identifying, 30–45, balances work and family, prioritizes wellness.
- **Motivations** Relaxation, pampering, consistent technicians, knowledge of safe products.
- **Goals** Book recurring appointments, receive maintenance tips, experience calming environment.
- **Pain Points** Overly busy salons, harsh chemicals, lack of personalization.
- **Key Messaging** “Indulge in mindful moments and gentle care tailored to your nail health.”
- **Preferred Channels** Email newsletters, wellness blogs, word of mouth, loyalty programs.

## Persona 4: The Social Influencer
- **Demographics** 20–29, content creator, 20k+ followers, collaborates with lifestyle brands.
- **Motivations** Unique, head-turning designs; behind-the-scenes footage; brand partnerships.
- **Goals** Exclusive looks, fast turnarounds, cross-promotional opportunities.
- **Pain Points** Generic designs, limited photo/video support, lack of influencer-friendly policies.
- **Key Messaging** “Co-create trending nail looks with a glam squad that supports your content calendar.”
- **Preferred Channels** Instagram DMs, TikTok, YouTube, PR outreach.

## Accessibility & Inclusion Considerations
- Offer accessible booking options (phone, chat) for clients with disabilities.
- Ensure clear communication of services for non-native English speakers; prepare translations if needed.
- Provide gender-inclusive messaging and imagery.
